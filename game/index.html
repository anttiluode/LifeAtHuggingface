          Life²

          A family, through history. Love is still a flux.

          You live one life at a time, from 18 until it ends. Every year you pour a few drops of attention into the people around you, your work, yourself. What you stop feeding withers by itself. Plagues, wars, famines and crashes arrive on their real dates. The family fortune can grow for centuries or vanish in a year. When you die, you choose which of your children carries the line on. When there is no one left, the line ends.

          Where does your family begin?

          Everything runs in your browser. With your own local models you can have every year narrated and the milestones painted: see “AI”.
